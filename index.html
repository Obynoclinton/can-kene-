About Us

Cannon Kene Sickle Cell Foundation was formed by Charity Amarachukwu (nee Aneke) after the death of her beloved cousin Cannon Kenechukwu Nwanyawu who died in June 2006 after a long battle with sickle cell disorder.Read More
The foundation is in loving memory of Cannon Kenechukwu Nwanyawu(1972-2006).
