About Us

Cannon Kene Sickle Cell Foundation was formed by Charity Amarachukwu (nee Aneke) after the death of her beloved cousin Cannon Kenechukwu Nwanyawu who died in June 2006 after a long battle with sickle cell disorder.Read More
The foundation is in loving memory of Cannon Kenechukwu Nwanyawu(1972-2006).

What we Do

Awareness

We raise awareness to dispel the myths about sickle cell disorder and highlight valuable information about the disorder. We achieve this through educational, counselling and screening programmes.

Advocacy

We support the course of people living with or who are affected by sickle cell disorder.

Support

We provide aid and support for meaningful living and improvement of quality of life for people living with Sickle cell disorder.

Research

We support research aimed at finding means of alleviation of burden for those living with sickle cell disorder.
